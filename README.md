## Case study (no code: it belongs to my employer)

**UAT evidence on autopilot.** For user acceptance testing of a government e-services platform, every catalog needed screenshots of a full request path: customer portal, back office, test payment, digital signature, certificate. Doing it by hand took days per service. I designed a Python + Playwright pipeline, built with an AI coding agent, that drives the request on staging, captures every screen and builds the print-ready PDF on the client-approved template. Preparation went from days to hours, and every catalog looks the same.

## How I use AI

I design the process and the rules; the AI agent writes much of the code; I review and test everything. Sensitive data stays local (client audio is transcribed offline), nothing is sent anywhere without a human approving it, and secrets never go into files.
